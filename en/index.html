2025 – 2026 · Master's thesis with Mamadou Diouhé Barry · Sorbonne Université · ANR BHAI project

Recognising characters in Byzantine seal monograms

The problem. A Byzantine monogram superimposes several Greek letters into a single design. Reading one takes an expert, and there are tens of thousands of seals.

Seal image→YOLOv8s detection→Crops→ResNet18 classification→Letters
  • Detection: mAP@50 0.778 — 351 images, 49 classes, 5-fold cross-validation
  • Classification: 96.7% accuracy, macro-F1 0.937 — 2,143 crops, 26 classes

Hate speech detection in French

The trap. The corpus has 42,660 rows but only 7,499 distinct texts: without deduplication, the test set simply repeats the training set. After a data audit, a weighted loss and a threshold tuned on validation data, CamemBERT reaches an F1 of 0.83 on the “hateful” class.
