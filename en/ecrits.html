Writing — Mots & Pouvoir

Decoding the world through language: politics, philosophy and literature.

As a computational linguist, I am as interested in how machines process language as in how language shapes power.

Mots & Pouvoir (“Words & Power”) is a French-language publication I write on Substack. It asks what words do to people: how speeches build authority, what languages say about societies, and what African thought brings to these questions.

This writing is the counterpart of my technical work. When I train a model to classify speech or to read inscriptions, the question of what language does is never far away.
